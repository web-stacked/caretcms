A logo that looks current in 2026 and dated in 2028 is a liability you paid for.
Durable identities tend to share a few traits: a clear idea, restraint with
effects, and type that was chosen rather than defaulted.

We design the system, not just the artefact — the spacing rules, the editorial
voice, the way the brand behaves when it's tired and shipping at midnight.

1. Decide what stays fixed for five years.
2. Decide what's allowed to flex by season.
3. Write it down so the next person can hold the line.

The goal isn't to be timeless — nothing is. It's to age slowly and on purpose.
